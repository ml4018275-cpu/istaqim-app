---
name: Islamic content sourcing
description: Authenticity and citation requirements for religious content in Istaqim.
---

Do not publish Qur'an commentary, hadith, fiqh rulings, fatwas, or Seerah reports as authoritative unless the source and reference can be verified. Preserve hadith chains when the user requests them; never invent quotations, chains, or claims of completeness.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires authentic Islamic material and reliable references.

**How to apply:** Keep sections marked as awaiting a trusted source until source material has been verified and cited.
